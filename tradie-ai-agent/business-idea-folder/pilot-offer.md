# 30-Day Pilot — Tradie Connect

**For:** [Business name]  **Trades:** [plumbing / electrical / …]  **Tradies:** [n]

## What you get
- An AI booking line (Australian phone number) that answers 24/7 and books jobs
- A crew line for your tradies: schedule, job completion, trip log
- The tradie portal (jobs + trip log) for you and your crew
- Setup of your tradie roster: name, trade, suburbs covered, 6-digit codes

## What we need from you
1. Tradie list: name, mobile, trade, suburbs each one covers
2. Decide which calls to forward: after-hours only, overflow, or all
3. Last month's rough missed-call count (for the before/after comparison)

## Timeline
| Day | Step |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Deploy (your AWS account or ours, Sydney region), load roster, test calls |
| 3 | Go live: forward calls |
| 3–30 | Run. Weekly 15-min check-in reviewing booked jobs |
| 30 | Review: jobs booked, calls handled, what to change. Decide on plan |

## Success measures
- Number of jobs booked by the AI that would otherwise have been missed
- % of calls completed without a human
- Tradie feedback on the crew line

## Current limits (so there are no surprises)
- No SMS notifications yet; tradies check the crew line or portal
- No live transfer to a person
- The AI doesn't quote prices; your tradie does
