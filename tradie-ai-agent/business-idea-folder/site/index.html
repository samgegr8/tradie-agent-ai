The problem

Small trade businesses lose work at the phone

On the tools, off the phone

A plumber under a house or an electrician up a ladder can't pick up. The caller rings the next business on Google.

After-hours calls go to voicemail

Emergencies happen at night and on weekends. A receptionist or answering service is costly for a 1 to 10 person crew.

Admin eats the evening

Writing up job details, working out who goes where, and keeping a vehicle logbook for tax time all happen after a full day's work.

What we sell

Three pieces, one system

All three are built and deployable today on the customer's own AWS account.

For your customers

The booking line

The AI answers, then collects:

  • Name and callback number
  • Trade needed and the problem
  • Full address with suburb and postcode
  • Preferred day and time
  • Emergency or standard

It creates the job card, finds a tradie who covers that trade and suburb, assigns them, and reads the job reference back to the caller.

● Live
For your tradies

The crew line

Tradies call in and say their 6-digit code. Then they can ask:

  • "What jobs have I got tomorrow?"
  • "Mark the Newtown job done."
  • "I'm heading to Bunnings Auburn."
  • "I've arrived." (ends the trip)
  • "Read me today's trip log."

Hands-free from the ute. Jobs can only be closed by the tradie they belong to.

● Live
For the office and the crew

The tradie portal

A simple web page. Log in with the same 6-digit code to see:

  • My Jobs: date, time, customer, address, description
  • Trip Log: every trip with type, destination, start and end time, filterable by day

Phone numbers never reach the browser. The code is the only login.

Questions prospects will ask

Will my customers know it's a robot?

Probably, and most won't mind if the job gets booked on the first call. The agent is friendly, keeps answers short and asks one question at a time.

Does it quote prices?

No, by design. It tells the caller the tradie will provide a quote. That keeps pricing in the tradie's hands.

What if no tradie covers that suburb?

The job is still logged with a reference number, and the caller is told someone will call back within 2 hours. Nothing is lost.

Can it put the caller through to me?

Not today. It is fully automated. A transfer-to-human option is a sensible add-on for the Crew tier.

Does my tradie get a text when a job is booked?

Not yet. The system already records each notification as pending, and SMS via Twilio is the next roadmap item. Until then tradies check the crew line or the portal.

Is the logbook ATO-compliant?

It records trip purpose, destination and times. Odometer readings, which a full ATO logbook needs, are on the roadmap. Position it as a logbook helper for now.
